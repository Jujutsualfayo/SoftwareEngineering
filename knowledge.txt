A Binary Search Tree is a special type of binary tree that follows a specific ordering rule:
- Values smaller than a node go to its left subtree.
- Values greater than a node go to its right subtree.
For a BST:
- Balanced tree: \(O(\log n)\)
- Skewed tree: \(O(n)\)
More precisely, searching takes \(O(h)\), where \(h\) is the height of the tree.
